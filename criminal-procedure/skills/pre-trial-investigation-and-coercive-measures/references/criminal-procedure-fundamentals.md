# Criminal procedure fundamentals

**Verified at source (Finlex, 2026-06):** the statute numbers below and the existence of the
proposed judgment procedure (tuomioesitys) in Chapter 5b ROL have been checked in Finlex. Always
verify the elements of offences, sentencing ranges, limitation periods and conditions for
coercive measures at the time of use with the `legal-core:legal-research` skill — not from this
file or from memory.

## Key statutes

- **Criminal Code (rikoslaki 39/1889, "RL")** — offences and their elements, general provisions on
  sentencing and limitation.
- **Pre-trial Investigation Act (esitutkintalaki 805/2011, "ETL")** — conducting the pre-trial
  investigation, the position and rights of parties, questioning and the final statement
  (loppulausunto).
- **Coercive Measures Act (pakkokeinolaki 806/2011, "PKL")** — apprehension, arrest, detention,
  travel bans, searches, seizure and covert coercive measures.
- **Act on Criminal Procedure (laki oikeudenkäynnistä rikosasioissa 689/1997, "ROL")** — right to
  prosecute, application for a summons, response, main hearing, proposed judgment under Chapter 5b,
  and claims by the injured party.
- **Code of Judicial Procedure (oikeudenkäymiskaari 4/1734, "OK")** — evidence under Chapter 17 and
  general procedural rules where applicable.
- **Legal Aid Act (oikeusapulaki 257/2002)** — public legal aid; ROL provisions on appointing
  defence counsel and counsel for the injured party.
- **Tort Liability Act (vahingonkorvauslaki 412/1974)** and **Crime Damage Act
  (rikosvahinkolaki 1204/2005)** — damages and state compensation for victims of crime.
- **Act on Restraining Orders (laki lähestymiskiellosta 898/1998)**.

## Procedural path

```
Criminal complaint (rikosilmoitus) → pre-trial investigation (police; lead investigator)
  → final statements (loppulausunnot) → consideration of charges (prosecutor)
  → charges / decision not to prosecute / restriction → (proposed judgment, Chapter 5b)
  → main hearing in the district court (käräjäoikeus) (or written procedure)
  → judgment → Court of Appeal (hovioikeus) (leave for continued consideration in some cases)
  → KKO (leave to appeal)
```

The summary fine procedure (sakkomenettely) follows a separate path in minor cases.

## Roles

- The **lead investigator (tutkinnanjohtaja)**, normally the police and in some cases the prosecutor,
  leads the pre-trial investigation; the **prosecutor (syyttäjä)** considers and prosecutes charges.
- The **suspect / defendant (epäilty / vastaaja)** — right to counsel at every stage, privilege
  against self-incrimination and right to be informed of the suspicion.
- The **injured party (asianomistaja)** — the victim of crime: right to counsel and a support person
  (retrieve the conditions at source), civil claims and the secondary right to prosecute.
- **Defence counsel / trial counsel (puolustaja / oikeudenkäyntiavustaja)** — eligibility and
  appointment under ROL and the Legal Aid Act.

## Procedural principles

- **Presumption of innocence (syyttömyysolettama)** and **privilege against self-incrimination
  (itsekriminointisuoja)** (see CLAUDE.md).
- **In dubio pro reo** — uncertainty in the evidence must favour the defendant; conviction requires
  that no reasonable doubt remains.
- **Principle of correspondence between the charge and the judgment (syytesidonnaisuus)** — the
  court may convict only for the act described in the charge.
- **Impartiality of the pre-trial investigation (esitutkinnan tasapuolisuus)** — matters supporting
  the suspect must also be investigated; the suspect may request investigative measures.
- **Proportionality and the principle of least harm** in the use of coercive measures.

## Map of coercive measures

| Coercive measure | Decision-maker | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Apprehension (kiinniottaminen) | police | short-term; retrieve the times at source |
| Arrest (pidättäminen) | official entitled to make an arrest | retrieve the conditions and times at source |
| Detention (vangitseminen) | court | retrieve the time limits for hearing the request and rehearing at source |
| Travel ban and enhanced travel ban (matkustuskielto, tehostettu matkustuskielto) | varies | alternative to detention |
| General or special search of premises and search of a place (yleinen/erityinen kotietsintä, paikanetsintä) | varies; the core area of domestic privacy receives enhanced protection | a search representative (etsintävaltuutettu) is used in certain situations |
| Seizure and copying (takavarikko ja jäljentäminen) | varies | admissibility and confidentiality considered separately |
| Covert coercive measures (salaiset pakkokeinot), including telecommunications interception | mainly the court | strict conditions; retrieve notification duties at source |

Decisions on coercive measures and their lawfulness may be referred to the court for review —
retrieve the remedies at source, including rehearing a detention matter and referring a search
for court examination.

## Typical risk points

- Attending questioning without counsel and without preparation.
- Omitting the final statement — it is an effective opportunity to influence the matter before
  consideration of charges.
- Failing to present the injured party's compensation claims in time.
- Using sentencing ranges, limitation periods or deadlines from memory.
- Disclosing investigation information to outsiders.
