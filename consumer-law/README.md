# Consumer law

Finnish consumer law governing transactions between traders and consumers:
liability for defects in goods and services, the right of withdrawal in distance
and off-premises selling, lawful marketing, the resolution of consumer disputes,
and good practice in collecting consumer debts.

> **Drafts are for review — not legal advice.**
> The Consumer Protection Act is **mandatory in favour of the consumer**: a term
> that weakens the consumer's statutory rights is void. The law changes frequently
> following EU directives, so chapters, deadlines and maximum collection charges
> must always be obtained from a source. See [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md).

## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| **consumer-sales-liability** | Assesses defects in goods and services and applies the consumer's remedies in order (repair or replacement, price reduction, termination of the sale and damages); drafts complaints; and explains the relationship between a guarantee and statutory liability for defects under the Consumer Protection Act (kuluttajansuojalaki 38/1978). Includes the consumer-law fundamentals reference. |
| **distance-selling-and-withdrawal** | Covers pre-contract information for distance and off-premises selling, the consumer's right of withdrawal and its exceptions, withdrawal forms and instructions, and assessment of unfair and misleading marketing. |
| **consumer-disputes-and-collection** | Covers dispute-resolution routes from consumer advisory services to the Consumer Disputes Board under the Act on the Consumer Disputes Board (laki kuluttajariitalautakunnasta 8/2007) and the courts, as well as good practice in collecting consumer debts, collection charges and payment demands under the Act on the Collection of Debts (laki saatavien perinnästä 513/1999), and default interest under the Interest Act (korkolaki 633/1982). |

## Verified foundational legislation

Verified at source (Finlex): the **Consumer Protection Act (kuluttajansuojalaki
38/1978)**, **Act on the Consumer Disputes Board (laki kuluttajariitalautakunnasta
8/2007)**, **Act on the Finnish Competition and Consumer Authority (laki Kilpailu-
ja kuluttajavirastosta 661/2012)**, **Act on the Collection of Debts (laki saatavien
perinnästä 513/1999)** and **Interest Act (korkolaki 633/1982)**. When using these
skills, check chapter contents, deadlines and maximum collection charges with the
`legal-core:legal-research` skill and at kkv.fi.

## Related domains

- **`contracts` domain** – B2B transactions and transactions between private
  individuals (Sale of Goods Act (kauppalaki) and general contract law); the
  Consumer Protection Act does not apply to them.
- **`insolvency` domain** – debt collection, limitation and enforcement generally.
- **`dispute-resolution` domain** – taking a dispute to the District Court.
- **`data-protection` domain** – direct marketing and processing personal data.
- **`legal-core` domain** – `legal-research` (practice of the Consumer Disputes
  Board and KKO).
